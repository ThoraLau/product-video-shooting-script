# Quality Gates

## Six professional judgments

### 1. Subject prominence

The product separates from the background through brightness, color, focus, scale, or movement. Hands, models, props, and graphics do not compete with it.

### 2. Visual depth

Foreground, midground, and background clarify the scene when useful. The sequence varies near/far, large/small, sharp/soft, bright/dark, or static/moving.

### 3. Light quality

Light has direction and reveals shape, material, stitching, water, texture, deformation, or edge separation. Important detail remains readable on dark and bright products.

### 4. Shot variation

Shot size, angle, camera height, focal treatment, and movement combine purposefully. The video does not stay at one eye-level angle.

### 5. Persuasive content

Each selling point is demonstrated, compared, measured, detailed, installed, loaded, stressed, or used. Claims do not exceed specifications, reviews, or visible evidence.

### 6. Editing rhythm

The opening communicates tension or value immediately. The middle escalates rather than listing features. Demonstrations remain on screen long enough to understand. The ending provides a result, memory point, or conversion action.

## Final acceptance

- Every row contains one consumer message and one visible proof.
- English voiceover is idiomatic, speakable, and synchronized.
- Product names and compound identifiers remain intact.
- Required product states, locations, props, and test conditions are reproducible.
- No unsupported safety, compatibility, capacity, durability, load, or weather claim remains.
- The Markdown and Excel deliverables are content-identical.
